VERIFIED CASE STUDY // MANCHESTER, TENNESSEE

Rapha Center

COMMERCIAL TPO RESTORATION & MOISTURE REMEDIATION

SQUARE FOOTAGE: 10,500 sq. ft. WARRANTY: 20-Year Warranted Roof System

Project Overview

The Rapha Center commercial facility in Manchester, TN required a rapid-turnaround roofing solution to resolve persistent roof leaks and upgrade overall energy efficiency.

Original Roof Condition & Engineering Challenges

Unchecked chronic water intrusion had saturated localized insulation pockets, requiring meticulous thermal moisture verification to avoid unnecessary total replacement of dry insulation zones.

Scope of Work Performed

  • • Full strip of deteriorated existing membrane down to insulation substrate.
  • • Diagnostic moisture inspection and targeted surgical extraction of wet polyiso boards.
  • • Installation of new high-density insulation fill and smooth coverboard substrate.
  • • Installation of mechanically attached 0.60 mil white reflective TPO single-ply membrane.
  • • Perimeter edge metal termination and heat-welded flashing wraps around all mechanical penetrations.

Verified Warranty & Long-Term Performance

Covered by a 20-Year Warranted Single-Ply Roof System ensuring long-term watertight reliability.
